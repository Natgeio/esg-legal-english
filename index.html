ESG Shifting Tides

2026-05-30 | Harvard Law School Forum on Corporate Governance

📖 英文原文摘要

The Environmental, Social, and Governance ("ESG") framework, once a dominant feature across corporate governance and sustainable finance, has declined in prominence, retreating from both corporate disclosures and investor focus.
At the disclosure level, aggregate "sustainability" keyword mentions in S&P 500 DEF 14A proxy filings peaked in 2024 and registered their first decline in 2025.

🎯 LSAT长难句拆解

This report quantifies that retreat through two lenses: (1) the elimination and re-branding of ESG terminology in S&P 500 and Fortune 1000 DEF 14A proxy and Form 10-K filings, and (2) the sustained capital exodus from ESG-designated mutual funds and ETFs.

中文： 本报告通过两个维度量化了这一撤退：(1)消除和重新命名ESG术语，(2)资金持续从ESG基金撤离。

Together, these illuminate the impact of recent regulatory, political, and market developments on the ESG framework, indicating that the tides are turning against ESG narratives as well as ESG investment.

中文： 这些数据揭示了近期监管、政治和市场发展对ESG框架的影响，潮流正在转向。
